Eligibility: original multivariate D≥2, numeric finite observations and binary anomaly labels, documented source trace identity and train cutoff, at least 320 initial-prefix observations and 256 test observations. Require the entire original training prefix to be normal, as verified by the evaluator, without moving its boundary or mining normal points from test. Split that prefix chronologically at floor(0.8*n_train), giving ≥256 fit and ≥64 calibration observations. Do not require a test anomaly or a measured performance level to qualify. Constant channels remain recorded; do not remove them based on test behavior.

Group exact duplicate raw feature traces and documented same-origin/cropped traces before selection; a group can supply at most one of the 12 slots. Exclude groups overlapping the two fixed SMD machines. Unknown provenance must be resolved, not presumed independent. Within each bucket order eligible filenames lexicographically. Choose the lexicographically smallest concatenation of four internally sorted three-file lists that satisfies all 12 distinct-source constraints (global feasible assignment, not greedy selection). Multi-tag series occupy one selection bucket but retain all tags for descriptive reporting.

If fewer than three eligible sources in any bucket, or no feasible 12-source assignment, STOP phase A and report exact exclusions. Do not replace with univariate, synthetic, contaminated-prefix series or altered buckets. Manifest fields: selection bucket, original tags, source group, URL, upstream commit, raw SHA256, metadata SHA256, D, N, cutoff, fit/calibration intervals, exclusion/qualification reason. Record the full sorted candidate inventory and exclusions so selection is reproducible.

Real datasets support anomaly detection/admission analysis. No real-data drift-vs-anomaly probe labels or exact post-shift latency are invented. These metrics are N/A unless independent annotations already exist and are frozen before model results. Synthetic-family transfer is not described as real-world cross-dataset validation.

## Synthetic and causal evaluation contract

D=8; five scenarios: stationary+anomaly, abrupt mean/scale, gradual, A→B→A, correlation shift. Use non-diagonal stable VAR(1) with correlated innovations; spectral radius 0.8, diagonal variances normalized, except for the explicitly fixed correlation-only construction below. Fit/calibration/test lengths 4096/1024/16384, burn-in 1024 excluded. Five detector seeds 11,22,33,44,55. Generator families use disjoint matrix/innovation seeds: 1000–1009 probe train, 2000–2004 probe validation, 3000–3009 test; each seed generates all five scenarios. Same source seed and its counterfactual variants never cross folds.

Correlation-only construction: let P be the cyclic 8-channel permutation, A=0.8P for both regimes, mean zero, target stationary covariance Sigma(rho)=(1−rho)I+rho*11^T, rho_A=0.1 and rho_B=0.5. Define Q_r=Sigma_r−A Sigma_r A^T=0.36 Sigma_r and innovations N(0,Q_r). Thus both regimes have identical stationary per-channel mean zero/variance one and different cross-channel correlations, with non-diagonal temporal dependence. Use the same A and intercept throughout. The finite transition covariance is a convex combination of the two target covariances, so diagonal variance remains one even across the switch; no state reset at a regime boundary. Initialize the anomaly-free latent process from N(0,Sigma_A). Apply anomalies to the observation stream, not the clean latent state.

Pre-generation deterministic float64 tests: symmetry absolute error≤1e-12; minimum eigenvalues of Sigma and Q>1e-8; spectral radius<1; discrete-Lyapunov residual max-absolute≤1e-10; independently solved stationary covariance matches target and regime diagonal differences≤1e-10; stationary means differ≤1e-10; maximum off-diagonal correlation difference≥0.2 (design value 0.4). Propagate covariance through all 256 transition steps and require diagonal deviation from one≤1e-10. Test fixed-seed channel permutations against the same invariants. Commit these executable tests before running any generator output inspection. Sample moments are reported as finite-sample diagnostics, never used to accept/reject seeds or renormalize test data. Any deterministic failure stops this scenario; a versioned rename to mixed covariance/scale shift is required before proceeding without these guarantees.

Each scenario includes spike, collective, dependency anomalies, plus fixed equal-type mixture as a separately reported condition. Severity levels 1,2,3 in initial-normal standard deviation units; anomaly event durations 1 for spikes and 16,64,256 for collective/dependency. Include short legitimate excursions with the latter durations and matching mean/correlation perturbations, plus persistent faults. Onset schedules, balanced class counts, channels, overlap, and generation equations must be committed in generator configuration before training; never tune them on detector outcomes. Include stationary-no-anomaly and identical-observation/opposite-semantic-label controls. The latter has exactly paired identical features and is expected to be non-identifiable.

Metadata stores regime, drift-active interval, anomaly interval/type, event IDs and overlap separately. Probe drift class comprises anomaly-free windows within the first 256 samples of a regime change or active gradual transition; anomaly class comprises windows containing anomaly. Mixed drift/anomaly windows are a separate stress stratum, not double-labeled binary training data. Stable new-normal and stationary-normal specificity are reported separately. Evaluate duration/severity-matched subsets and natural-prevalence results separately.

Common causal track: window 64, stride 1, decision timestamp at right edge. Scalar window reconstruction score is evaluated against window-any-anomaly label; endpoint point scores and point labels are separate metrics, never mixed. No retrospective score filling. Score current input using pre-update state, emit immutable output, then select/update; any normalizer update occurs after the score. Initial fit data sets scaler; calibration fixes 95th-percentile threshold and references (no test-best threshold). Official reproduction may have different native semantics, retained and labeled.

Adaptation API contains x/time/algorithm state only. Labels/event boundaries are evaluator-only. Audit must establish label permutation cannot change scores, selections or updates. Exclude prefix/warmup/padding from test metrics. All model/optimizer/RNG state resets at independent run boundaries.

Metrics: primary average precision (AP); also trapezoidal PR-AUC explicitly named for CANDI reproduction, AUROC, unadjusted recall/FPR, optional VUS-PR with fixed implementation and buffer range 0–64. No point adjustment. Report per-series macro summaries and anomaly prevalence. Undefined metrics stay N/A with denominators.

Post-shift horizon: 256 samples from transition start, clean normal endpoints only. Sustained recovery: first of three consecutive non-overlapping 64-sample blocks with normal-point FPR≤0.10; require ≥32 clean endpoints per block, otherwise censored. Log candidate-to-commit, shift-to-first-update, shift-to-recovery and alarm latency separately; unavailable real boundaries are N/A.

## H1 causal contamination intervention and delay

For each anomaly type and equal mixture, c={0,5,10,20,30}% refers to anomaly-window proportion in a 100-window update buffer. Pair runs on identical initial checkpoint, RNG, normal counterpart windows and subsequent evaluation stream. Replace exactly 0/5/10/20/30 buffer entries with matched corrupted counterparts. Oracle access is confined to buffer composition; the update operator is the pinned official CANDI SANA operator, not generic backbone fine-tuning. Use the same frozen backbone, SANA input/output modules, official calculate_loss, optimizer class and every resolved setting (including learning rate, momentum, weight decay, steps), model train/eval handling and initialized optimizer/model state. Clone the entire pre-intervention state for clean and contaminated arms; keep buffer count, order, steps and exposures identical. Do not replace the official configured step count with a universal one-step rule. Pair each fixed SMD script configuration separately; the synthetic primary operator uses the pinned machine-1-8 alpha=5 script settings, with only input shape/dataset wiring changed and recorded before execution. The clean-buffer audit overlay must match an official operator call on the same buffer/state before H1 runs; any compatibility patch is separate from unchanged reproduction.

This tests clean SANA update versus contaminated SANA update. Evaluate on an identical subsequent held-out stream before additional updates; report no-update controls. Match normal regime/age and anomaly duration/severity, preserve counterfactual source grouping. All c levels are reported, but c=30% is stress-only and cannot independently unlock H1-harm GO. A qualifying effect must occur at c∈{5,10,20}% or in the prespecified fixed-SMD natural-contamination audit.

Natural-harm alternative: independently record actual committed SMD candidate buffers. In a separate evaluator-controlled counterfactual audit, compare original contaminated SANA updates with equally sized clean counterpart buffers drawn chronologically from previously available evaluator-verified normal candidates; match hard/moderate queue, window age and update schedule, log substitutions and matching quality. Never use future normal samples, and never feed this oracle into deployment. If clean matches are unavailable, mark this alternative N/A rather than changing the schedule. Require AP(clean)−AP(natural)≥0.02, paired CI lower bound>0, Holm correction across the two machines × three alpha settings, and positive direction in ≥4/5 seeds. This supports causal harm conditional on matching assumptions, not merely nonzero admission or static-versus-adaptive differences. All matching rules must be executable and committed before that audit's outcomes are read.

H4b only: keep K={0,4,8,16,32}; K counts decision windows, not selected candidates. K=0 is no extra delay on top of official MIN_SAMPLES=16 hard/moderate queues. An arrival at t matures at t+K; score first, then append matured candidates in original selection order to original queues and preserve hard-before-moderate update order. Do not revalidate or reject. Track age plus original batch latency, and do not flush tail using future data. The common arrival cutoff T−32 controls minimum maturity; separately report mature-but-uncommitted samples waiting for MIN_SAMPLES. For a fixed candidate cohort eventually fully committed, FIFO preserves its contamination fraction exactly. Report counts for selected, matured, batch-eligible, committed and pending cohorts; maturity alone is not a guarantee of a final optimizer update.

Fixed-update-budget diagnostic replays the same prespecified candidate batches/step counts on a common future evaluation segment; changing timing does not authorize more training or different examples. Pure FIFO may change endogenous selection through model trajectory, but no claim of decontamination follows just from fewer tail commits.

## H4a future-evidence value diagnostic

Ambiguous candidates are the pinned CANDI hard FPM candidates, selected using x[≤t] and fixed initial calibration only. Primary diagnostic uses a frozen CANDI trajectory with no intervening updates and fixes candidate IDs at t for every K; no reselection based on t+K or label. Restrict all K and current-only comparisons to the same candidates with a full t+32 follow-up. This is a new offline information diagnostic, not a change to official CANDI or an online learned gate. An adaptive-trajectory replication may be secondary, explicitly confounded by intervening updates.

At t+K only observed scores/residuals, causal derivatives/persistence, hidden/state deltas and available gate/memory summaries through that timestamp may enter features. Do not read beyond t+K; do not use labels, event age/end, regime identity or generator parameters. Ground-truth class is the candidate's status at t, not its later status. Mixed/unknown drift labels remain a separate stratum as in H2; real-data drift classification is N/A without independent annotation. In probe fitting only, the evaluator supplies training labels to the offline logistic learner; feature extraction and online algorithms never receive them.

Primary feature family is backbone-independent history: 14 current/history features already specified below plus fixed mean/std/linear slope/last-minus-first of scores over [t+1,t+K]. Its matched-capacity current-only arm substitutes the same four summaries over [t−K+1,t], so both have 18 columns and identical logistic regularization/selection budget. Train a separate probe per K with the existing source-disjoint folds; same candidate cohort, labels and prevalence for both arms. Report the original 14-feature control additionally. Hidden/state/gate/memory trajectories use the common H3a schema as prespecified secondary families; xLSTM extras are exploratory and cannot rescue a failed primary H4a. Thus no H3a-positive requirement is introduced into safe-adaptation continuation.

Use the H2 logistic/seed/bootstrap setup and Holm correction across four K. Duration, severity, event identity and time-position controls are mandatory evaluator-side checks: balance/match duration and severity, equal-weight events, stratify candidates by test-stream quartile, and preserve source/event grouping with purge expanded to 64+32+32=128 observations when temporal splitting is unavoidable. Controls may use metadata for evaluation/matching only. A qualifying K must pass the primary and matched-control analysis and retain positive aggregate gain in each of the four time-position strata; insufficient stratum support is inconclusive. No test-informed choice of matching bins or alternative feature family. Record exact feature availability times and a future-prefix invariance test before probe fitting.

## H2 / H3a probes and conditional H3b

Positive class anomaly; offline L2 logistic regression only. StandardScaler fitted on probe-train; C∈{0.01,0.1,1,10}, selected by validation AP, ties choose smaller C. Never choose layer, feature family or seed on test. Group by source realization/event; no random overlapping-window split. For any temporal split purge 64+32=96 observations on either side and keep complete events together.

Prespecified six groups: score/history control; hidden only; gate only; memory only; combined internal; history+combined. Confirmatory common schema has exactly 18 internal columns in each backbone: hidden mean/std/RMS/delta RMS (4); input and retention effective-gate mean/std/q10/q90 plus change in mean (5 each, 10 total); scalar memory readout mean/std/RMS/relative delta norm (4). Use scalar sLSTM c/n for xLSTM and c for LSTM, standardized per statistic on probe-train only. Relative delta is ||u_t−u_previous||/(||u_previous||+1e-8); within each window initialize differences from its own zero state and summarize causally at its right edge. Pool equally across eligible scalar recurrent layers/heads with no learned weights. Hidden/gates for this comparison come from the same scalar cells; exclude mLSTM-only statistics from confirmatory H3a. Differences in the meaning of effective gates and normalized cell states must be disclosed; equal column count does not prove complete architecture equivalence.

For every internal column append trailing mean/std/slope over 4,8,16,32 decisions: 18*(1+12)=234 internal columns each. Hidden-only=52, gate-only=130, memory-only=52; combined=234, history+combined=248. Score/history control is exactly 14 columns (current score, first difference, trailing mean/std/slope over 4,8,16,32). The incremental-gain comparison uses this identical history feature definition in both backbones; additionally report a shared frozen CANDI score/history control with each backbone's internal summaries to separate score-source differences. Identical logistic family, C grid, fit folds, feature standardization and selection budgets are mandatory. Missing scalar features block confirmatory H3a; no unequal zero-padding or backbone-specific replacement. Freeze the executable column schema in E before labeled feature extraction.

Additional mLSTM matrix/readout summaries, sLSTM n/stabilizer and raw logits remain logged as numerical audit or explicitly exploratory features. They cannot drive H3a GO in this protocol. Do not flatten matrices or promote an exploratory winner; any future matched-capacity experiment requires another pre-outcome amendment.

H3a is strictly window-local xLSTMAD reset. External rolling summaries have equal history budgets in LSTM/control. H3b is gated on H3a GO and is synthetic only: same frozen xLSTM weights, same non-overlapping 64-sample chunks, same input/history budget, reset at every chunk versus causal carry of all recurrent/conv states. Carry only within a source stream; no optimizer updates. Fresh probe fits use the existing train/validation/test partition. Do not compare carry with overlapping-window replay (which double-consumes observations). Reuse parity tests; no architecture change. This is a diagnostic, not xLSTMAD reproduction or a production detector.

## Decision rules frozen before outcomes

All differences are paired, macro across test source realizations; 5 detector seeds. Use 10,000 hierarchical bootstrap draws (seed 901), resample source realizations first and detector seeds second, retaining whole events/windows. Report 95% CIs. Use paired source-cluster sign-flip tests (exact enumeration when feasible, otherwise 10,000 permutations, seed 902), with Holm familywise 0.05 in addition to practical margins: controlled H1's 4 positive c × 4 anomaly conditions plus the six natural SMD alternatives form one 22-comparison family; unavailable natural comparisons use p=1. H4a has four K comparisons. H4b has no confirmatory decontamination test. H2/H3a combined comparisons are confirmatory; individual feature-family results are descriptive. Do not promote a winning ablation when the combined test fails.
