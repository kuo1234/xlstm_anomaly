# Decision

## `M1_REPLICATED_SIGNAL`

The 11/22/33 exploratory funnel shows a clear and reproducible mLSTM
increment beyond score/history.  All three frozen detector seeds are positive,
and all four shifted scenarios are positive for that M|H contrast.

The more stringent conditional contrast M|H+S is positive in all three pooled
seeds but heterogeneous by scenario and duration.  Therefore the decision is
not a claim that mLSTM is globally superior to sLSTM: it is a bounded
mechanism signal that merits a separately preregistered replication/robustness
study.  No automatic H3b, long-context, persistent-state, CUDA-training, or
five-seed experiment is started here.

Recommended next step: write a fresh confirmatory protocol for mLSTM
incremental information, with an explicit source/condition weighting rule and
scenario/duration robustness requirement.  Only after review should a larger
replication be considered.  The existing frozen G1/H3a/H1 conclusions remain
unchanged.
