# Future-dated leads

These records are **not used as current evidence** for any status decision. They are listed so that the novelty case can be re-checked before submission.

## What counts as future-dated here

None of the six literature tracks found a record whose first public availability (online-first date or preprint date) is after 2026-09-26. arXiv preprints cannot post-date the search date. The future-dated items below are publisher records whose **issue or volume date is after 2026-09-26**, although CrossRef/OpenAlex list an online-first date before the cutoff. The prior audit also treated items available online before the cutoff as admissible. Here they are kept separate, as the brief requires, because their final versions may change and several were seen only at title level.

The lead-agent sweep ran twelve CrossRef bibliographic queries restricted to publication dates from 2026-09-27 to 2027-12-31 (queries listed in [sources.md](sources.md)). It returned 135 records. 73 of them matched a topical keyword filter, and the 21 most relevant are listed. Online-first dates come from OpenAlex. Abstracts were available for 7 of the 21; the rest are title-level.
