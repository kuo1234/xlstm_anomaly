## Inference and limitations

- The equal-law classification lower bound is proved directly in identifiability_theorems_and_counterexamples.md.
- The NIST sources illustrate context and measurement references in manufacturing/metrology; they do not establish that the project's application has those fields.
- The domain-adaptation paper is relevant to explicit shared-hypothesis assumptions, not a direct fault-diagnosis theorem.
- No source can decide the semantics of an unopened project dataset. Context availability, label provenance, and real-world fault coverage remain unknown here.
- This is a targeted theoretical investigation, not an exhaustive systematic literature review.
