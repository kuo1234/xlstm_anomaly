- `ALREADY_EXPLICIT`: a read primary source directly states/evaluates the candidate failure or its generic framing.
- `PARTIALLY_STUDIED`: an explicit neighboring formulation exists, but the specific requested decomposition is not established by the inspected evidence.
- `NOT_FOUND_IN_SCOPE`: the bounded inspected sources/search did not establish the specific formulation; no absence or novelty claim follows.
- `UNKNOWN`: primary full text or relevant artifact was not inspected/available, or evidence cannot decide the question.

## Read primary sources

**[Exathlon, PVLDB 2021](https://people.csail.mit.edu/tatbul/publications/exathlon_vldb21.pdf)** — PDF pp4–5, 10–11 (journal pp2616–2617, 2622–2623), §§3.2, 4.1, 6.2. RCI records injected events; EEI reflects later effects, manually set with domain knowledge. AD1/AD2 evaluate their union; AD3 references RCI onset; AD4 penalizes duplicate detections. The experimental discussion explicitly identifies LSTM score discontinuities (R4/R7), global threshold difficulty (R5), and type/context variability (R2/R3). These observations already occupy F2–F4. Its AD and ED tasks differ: explanation discovery is not a phase detector. The inspected evaluation does not provide a separate RCI/EEI score-failure decomposition.

**[DIVAD, PVLDB 2025](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/~yanlei.diao/publications/VLDB2025_Jacob.pdf)** — author-hosted published PDF pp4, 8–10, §§3.2–3.3.2, 6.1–6.2.4. This explicitly studies domain shift among contexts of existing applications, defined by Spark settings and input rate. Its causal online wrapper assigns each sliding-window score to the arriving right-edge record and applies EWMA, controlling stability versus reactivity. Evaluation uses peak point F1 on the PR curve, with type-averaged recall and a smoothing grid; this is oracle separation evidence, not a deployable normal-calibrated alarm result. It reports normal train/test score distributions and contextual anomaly behavior, occupying F3/F4. No separate phase decomposition is established. The publisher URL timed out; the alternate is separately identified and hashed, not assumed identical.

**[Post-Inference Guided Transformer, MLSP 2025](https://blog.metu.edu.tr/ttemizel/files/2025/08/Post_Inference_Guided_Transformer_for_Anomaly_Interval_Localization_in_Multivariate_Time_Series.pdf)** — pp2–5, §§2–4, Tables2–3/Figure1. It extends Exathlon to multiclass interval detection and combines overlapping-prediction voting with domain-based normal↔anomaly transition masks. Trace-disjoint three-fold splits use disturbed traces for training/validation; inputs include anomaly-class history, so its supervision differs materially from normal-only AD. Binary AD1–AD4 and multiclass results explicitly cover continuity and type dependence. Transition masks can persist in an incorrect class; voting can suppress anomalies. This is substantial F2/F4 overlap, but does not establish RCI-versus-EEI performance. A thesis describing the same work is a duplicate lead, not independent replication.

**[MIXAD, ICPR 2024 preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.22735v1)** — p14, §5/Table3/Figure5. Its Exathlon case study uses application1 with two anomaly types and compares DuoGAT/TranAD. It distinguishes point-adjusted from unadjusted results and assesses explanation consistency by same/different event types. The paper calls these point-based AD1/AD2 adaptations; they are not interchangeable with native range-based AD1–AD4. Feature attribution and memory activation changes are not proof of injected-root-cause phase detection, temporal causality, or replicated lifecycle failure across applications.

**[xLSTMAD, ICDM 2025 preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.22837v2)** — pp5–8, §§IV–V/TableIII. Reconstruction/forecasting xLSTM variants with MSE/SoftDTW are evaluated on transformed TSB-AD-M series, including aggregate Exathlon VUS results. Thus xLSTM applied to Exathlon and shape-aware sequence comparison are already occupied. These aggregate metrics do not preserve or demonstrate native RCI/EEI failures. The November2025 v2 is the evidence version; downloaded June2025 v1 is retained only for provenance.

**[Cloud telemetry benchmark, 2026 preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13288v1)** — pp5–6, 11–12, 15, §§IV, V-C, VII. It compares GRU/TCN/Transformer/TSMixer/IsolationForest and explicitly separates calibration instability from detection, with Exathlon app5 versus app9 case studies and false-positive penalties. This directly occupies F3 and context-dependent calibration. Calibration optimizes NAB score on a validation slice within the training period; this does not establish Issue25's normal-only calibration. The authors note NAB sensitivity and absence of statistical significance testing. Its collapsed anomaly windows are not lifecycle labels.

**[mTSBench, TMLR 2026 / arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.21550v2)** — pp23–24, AppendixC/FiguresC.1–C.2. It discusses visually ambiguous Exathlon annotations and poor prediction overlap among five detectors in an example. This is a salience/annotation warning, not proof that injected RCI or expert EEI labels are false. Visual ambiguity cannot justify relabeling or invalidate ground-truth provenance. OpenReview returned HTTP403; the explicit official arXiv alternate is pinned and hashed.

## E0 interpretation and remaining uncertainty

Generic known failure is not a new semantic-failure contribution. Generic root-cause explanation, multiclass interval classification, domain generalization, and xLSTM use must not be relabeled as lifecycle novelty. A defensible residual would require measurements of repeated phase-specific failures across supported types/applications and multiple distinct baseline families, with raw score differences surviving normal-only alarm calibration and explicit smoothing/truncation/overlap/censoring controls. This audit supplies neither those measurements nor a research GO.

The bounded 2024–2026 search identified additional unreviewed hypergraph, robust detection and CRAFTIIF leads, recorded as `UNKNOWN` in the search log. Searches for Exathlon/Mamba returned substantial unrelated sports/vision noise; no negative claim about SSM/Mamba occupancy is justified. No exhaustive citation closure or comprehensive database screening was performed. A search miss cannot satisfy the "not completely prior-occupied" gate; evidence must first distinguish the narrow measured residual from the explicit overlaps above.
