No candidate's empirical shift rationale itself furnishes legitimate-change truth.

### C. Drift-vs-anomaly external validation

**No acquired public multivariate dataset reproduces the synthetic estimand** of anomaly-free active drift windows, anomaly windows, overlap, and stable new normal with independently timed labels. The best *narrow semantic pilot* is the two byte-verified AnDri Sensor/`real iot` files: they contain per-row anomaly_point/pattern and three source `change_point` markers, and the extended paper attributes these to expert annotation. Use them only for a separately specified, univariate **change-onset versus anomaly** feasibility question after seeking annotation/temporal-validity confirmation. Do not extrapolate three onsets into first-256-sample drift intervals or claim four-way overlap truth. V2 ECG/IOPS provide controlled injection stress if exact generated artifacts are recovered, explicitly labeled semi-synthetic; Elec/Weather natural drift has no frozen ground-truth timing. Keep C's exact real-world claim **N/A** unless new independent labels are obtained.
