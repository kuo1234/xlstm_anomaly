# Novelty matrix

This is a structured comparison of the papers most relevant to the proposed system. “NR” means the inspected source did not establish the property; it does not prove the method lacks it. For supervision, distinguish labels used to train/evaluate from labels available to online adaptation. “Normal-only target” means target initialization/adaptation data are explicitly selected as normal, not merely unlabeled mixed test data.

The first table covers architecture and method components. The second covers deployment conditions and remaining overlap. Paper names link to the primary record when available.

## Matrix interpretation

The candidate’s strongest direct novelty pressure is not “memory” alone. It is the combination of (i) source-pretrained recurrent forecast model, (ii) explicit target-normal sample-efficiency curve, and (iii) later semantic regime promotion. The first two can be tested independently; the third is blocked by observation-only identifiability and by the weak semantic labels of common TSAD benchmarks. The exact combination remains **PARTIALLY_OVERLAPPING**, not proven novel.
