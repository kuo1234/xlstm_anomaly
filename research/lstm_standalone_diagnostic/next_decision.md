# Next decision

The standalone LSTM increment is clearly reconstructed, so this diagnostic is
not a reason to repeat G1 or reopen H3b. The next useful experiment should be
a separately specified strong observable/residual control, not a post-hoc
change to the frozen H2/H3a protocol.

Before claiming architecture-specific recurrent evidence, a future study
should preserve source-level pairing and compare both backbones against the
same strong observable/residual baseline. A multi-seed dense mLSTM replication
remains exploratory and should be scheduled independently; this diagnostic
does not authorize it.
