# Interpretation

Classification: **LSTM_INTERNAL_SIGNAL_RECONSTRUCTED**.

Under the same frozen G1 feature/probe protocol, the matched conventional LSTM
also shows a large incremental internal-state signal beyond its own
reconstruction-score history. The direction is uniform across all 50
source/seed units and every seed/source macro mean exceeds the old `+0.02`
reference margin.

This changes the interpretation of H3a STOP: the frozen common scalar
comparison did not establish an xLSTM-specific advantage because the matched
LSTM has a comparably large standalone increment. It is not an equivalence
test and does not establish that the two models have identical behavior.

The result does **not** establish that all recurrent models contain this
information, that xLSTM has no benefit, safe adaptation, contamination
robustness, matrix-memory specificity, persistent-state benefit, or long-
context superiority. The post-G1 mLSTM evidence remains separate exploratory
evidence that may represent a complementary xLSTM channel outside the original
common scalar schema.

Scenario and shared-CANDI standalone LSTM effects cannot be recovered from the
compact committed artifacts and are reported as unavailable rather than
inferred.
