# Prior art around safe continual normality adaptation in non-stationary MTSAD

Three of the four components of "Safe Continual Normality Adaptation" already exist in the literature under other names, and each exists in a form strong enough to be cited as a baseline rather than as related work. Admission control on a normality buffer using a model-internal quantity is published TSAD practice ([Kim, Mok, Lee et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i17.38524); [COMET 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01635); [MemStream 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03837)). A sequential statistical test on a detector's own output stream, used to decide that a change is drift rather than an anomaly and to defer that decision until enough evidence accumulates, is published ([Bandyopadhyay, Bose & Roy Chowdhury 2025](https://doi.org/10.33889/IJMEMS.2025.10.3.039); [Han & Qu 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30502); [Zhu, Cai, Deng et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.14778/3636218.3636233)). Reset and restore triggered by drift evidence derived from the model's own signals is a solved sub-problem in continual test-time adaptation ([CoTTA 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13591); [RDumb 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05401); [PETAL 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09713); [adaptive selective reset, ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03796); [RDumb++ 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15544)). What is *not* occupied by anything retrieved here is the specific object the project's current probe is about: recurrent gate and memory dynamics treated as a measured information source for separating legitimate normality evolution from anomaly-like change, with the separation evaluated as a prediction problem against evaluator-side truth rather than asserted by construction — and, downstream of that, a delayed and reversible normality commitment whose contamination cost is accounted for causally rather than described as tolerable.

The honest summary of the novelty boundary is therefore narrow but real, and it is narrower than the project's long-term framing suggests. Anyone reading only the framing ("learn the new normal without learning the anomaly") will find that framing anticipated — most explicitly by [SCALE (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817912), which poses online latent-domain anomaly detection with unobserved shift times and separates domain drift from true anomalies with two decoupled model-derived statistics. What survives is a measurement claim and an accounting claim, not a framing claim.

## Adaptation under normality shift in multivariate TSAD

### F1 — CANDI and curated / selective test-time adaptation for TSAD

This family established that test-time adaptation of a frozen reconstruction detector is worth doing under normality shift, and that *which* windows you adapt on matters more than how many. [CANDI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i17.38524) selects adaptation windows by two internal criteria — anomaly score above a validation percentile, and small Mahalanobis distance in the frozen encoder's latent space to a reference set of high-scoring *normal validation* windows — and updates only a lightweight residual module (SANA) rather than the detector, reporting AUROC gains up to 14% with fewer adaptation samples. [TAFAS (AAAI 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i17.33965), from the same group, supplies the gated-calibration and periodicity-aware-adaptation machinery that CANDI's module is motivated by, in forecasting rather than detection. What the family does *not* establish is that curation prevents contamination: CANDI's own analysis states that adaptation is sometimes performed on mislabelled anomalous data and argues only that the method is "robust to moderate contamination", and it lists failure recovery as future work. Entry points: CANDI, then [COMET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01635) for the threshold-free variant of the same admission idea, then TAFAS for the module design.

### F2 — M2N2 and "new normal" adaptation

[M2N2 (AAAI 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i12.29210) is the reference point: it detrends with an EMA trend estimator and masks the test-time reconstruction loss by one minus its own predicted anomaly label, so the update is computed only over windows the detector currently believes are normal. That single line — the detector's own score decides what it learns from — is the field's default contamination guard, and it is what both CANDI and COMET position against. [COMET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01635) replaces the threshold with codebook-activation evidence: entries activated during normal-only training identify reliable normal samples, and the codebook is updated only on those "to prevent codebook contamination". [MemStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03837) had already gated memory updates by a discounted KNN score threshold and handled drift by memory replacement, with a proposition relating memory size to drift speed. The family does not establish that any of these guards is *sufficient*; none of them measures the causal cost of an admitted anomaly, and none defers an admission decision.

### F3 — Continual / streaming / online TSAD

The streaming line has converged on two structural answers to non-stationarity: maintain a pool or ensemble of models and route, or maintain a memory of normal patterns and replace. [ARCUS (KDD 2022)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3534678.3539348) monitors the reliability of an autoencoder pool against incoming points and either merges into an existing model or spawns a new one when the pool is judged inadequate, "presumably due to a concept drift". [METER (PVLDB 2023)](https://doi.org/10.14778/3636218.3636233) routes by evidential concept uncertainty between a static detector and a dynamically generated one. [ReCATS (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817985) is the replay-free continual MTSAD anchor, with multi-regime modelling, generative alignment, dual-scale thresholding, and BWT/FWT evaluation. The industrial-service literature ([Microsoft, KDD 2019](https://doi.org/10.1145/3292500.3330680)) documents the deployment constraints but describes no mechanism for excluding real anomalies from its update path. Entry points: ARCUS, METER, ReCATS.

### F5 — xLSTM, and xLSTM for anomaly detection

This family is thin by construction and the thinness is informative. [xLSTM (NeurIPS 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04517) introduces exponential gating and a matrix memory and makes no claim about anomaly detection, drift, or the information content of its internal states. [xLSTMAD (ICDM 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDM65498.2025.00032) is, in the retrieved set, the only xLSTM-based anomaly detector: an offline detector with no adaptation, no drift handling, and no use of internal state as evidence. Searches for xLSTM plus anomaly detection returned a single arXiv record. So the xLSTM-TSAD literature currently establishes only that xLSTM backbones are competitive detectors; there is no published claim about their gates carrying drift-relevant information to inherit, and equally no published evidence that they do.

## Drift, deferral and reversibility

### F4 — Concept-drift detection and drift-aware anomaly detection

Concept drift has its own mature vocabulary ([Gama et al., ACM CSUR 2014](https://doi.org/10.1145/2523813)) and its own critique of that vocabulary's inconsistency ([Bayram et al., KBS 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2022.108632), which also surveys *performance-aware* detectors that monitor model signals rather than input statistics). The most relevant instance for this project is [STAD (DKE 2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.datak.2024.102365): an autoencoder-based detector that maps each period of the stream to a discrete state, uses statistical tests (with a reported sensitivity/power analysis) to decide transitions, and thereby makes model adaptation under drift interpretable. [Drift explanations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15830) and [DriftGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08928) attack the complementary problem — attributing a detected drift to features or components — with permutation importance and SHAP respectively. Note the benchmark gap: the drift-aware TSAD literature retrieved here does not supply a public benchmark with per-timestamp drift-versus-anomaly ground truth; the named "StrAD / TSB-drift" anchors did not resolve to such a benchmark (see coverage section). Entry points: STAD, Bayram et al., DriftGuard.

### F8 — Quarantine, deferred decision, delayed commitment

Deferral is more thoroughly explored than the project's framing implies, but it is scattered across settings. [METER](https://doi.org/10.14778/3636218.3636233) accumulates concept uncertainty over a sliding window before performing an offline update — an accumulate-then-commit loop. The [HTM+SPRT framework](https://doi.org/10.33889/IJMEMS.2025.10.3.039) binarises the detector's own anomaly likelihood and runs a sequential probability ratio test over a lagged window, restarting the test after each drift decision, which is a deferred drift declaration built on internal evidence. [Quilt (AAAI 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09691) waits for a minimum number of post-drift samples before selecting training segments. [A drift-aware online MTSAD method (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09358) excludes samples in the current detection window from supervised updating and imposes a detection cooldown. [An RL + human-feedback detector (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08373) holds incomplete transitions in a pending buffer until expert validation and defaults to treating flagged anomalies as false positives unless validated. Stream-based active learning has formalised the delay itself as "verification latency" ([ICANN 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-15937-4_22)). What none of these does is quantify what the deferral bought in terms of contamination avoided.

### F9 — Rollback, reset and restore in continual test-time adaptation

This family is effectively solved for classification CTTA and should be treated as prior art rather than as an opportunity. [CoTTA (CVPR 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13591) stochastically restores a fraction of trainable weights to source values at every step; [EATA (ICML 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02610) combines entropy-based sample exclusion with Fisher-weighted anti-forgetting regularisation; [SAR (ICLR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12400) resets parameters when a moving average of entropy loss indicates collapse; [PETAL (CVPR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09713) restores parameters selected by the diagonal Fisher information matrix; [RDumb](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05401) shows that a fixed periodic reset to pretrained weights beats most of the field on long streams; [adaptive selective reset (ICLR 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03796) makes both *when* and *where* to reset a function of prediction concentration and its EMA; and [RDumb++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15544) triggers full or soft resets from entropy- and KL-based drift detectors. The open question these leave is not reset timing but reset *semantics* under unsupervised normality change: all of them reset toward a fixed source model, none of them reverses a *normality commitment* while preserving a legitimately learned new normal.

### F14 — Methods that explicitly distinguish anomaly from benign distribution shift

The cleanest statement of the distinction is conceptual: the [generalized OOD survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11334) separates covariate shift from semantic shift and notes that most anomaly-detection settings assume a clean in-distribution training set. Operationally, [SCALE (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817912) is the most direct: it separates domain drift from true anomalies online with Sparse Likelihood Regret for drift and Counterfactual Prediction Residual for anomaly scoring, with claimed theory for the separation. [STAD](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.datak.2024.102365) does it with statistical tests over reconstructions; the [HTM+SPRT framework](https://doi.org/10.33889/IJMEMS.2025.10.3.039) with a sequential test over binarised likelihoods; [anytime-valid gating](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30502) with a decision table over a conformal test martingale. [AnoShift (NeurIPS 2022 D&B)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15476) supplies a distribution-shift benchmark for unsupervised AD but evaluates degradation, not separation. The family establishes that the separation can be *operationalised*; it does not establish how much information any particular internal signal carries about it, because none of these papers measures the separation as a prediction problem against timestamp-level truth.

### F15 — Uncertainty-guided and conformal / sequential-test-guided adaptation

Statistical machinery for "wait for more evidence" is available off the shelf and is already being wired into adaptation loops. [A conformal uncertainty indicator for CTTA (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02998) supplies a testing-time uncertainty estimate and adapts more aggressively on samples with reliable predictions. [Anytime-valid gating (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30502) uses a filter's own standardised innovation as the nonconformity score of a conformal test martingale and maps the martingale's state onto a four-way action table with reset-on-fire. [Segmented confidence sequences (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06638) give time-uniform thresholds for streaming detection with coverage guarantees under arbitrary stopping. [METER](https://doi.org/10.14778/3636218.3636233) uses evidential (Dirichlet) uncertainty as the routing signal. The family establishes validity and gating; it does not establish that any of these gates improves *anomaly-detection* outcomes under contamination, and the martingale paper in particular reports no detection-quality evaluation.

## Selection, memory and continual anomaly detection

### F7 — Selective adaptation, selective update, sample selection

Selection by model-internal confidence is the dominant pattern: entropy-based sample exclusion plus Fisher regularisation ([EATA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02610)), reliability filtering with sharpness-aware updates ([SAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12400)), instance-wise BN statistics with a prediction-balanced reservoir against temporal correlation ([NOTE, NeurIPS 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05117)), high- and low-quality sample partitioning with positive/negative learning ([DSS, WACV 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03335)), and the finding that entropy alone is insufficient under spurious-correlation shift, motivating disentangled-factor criteria ([DeYO, ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07366)) and entropy+gradient-norm combinations ([ETAGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09251)). The family establishes that internal confidence signals are usable admission criteria and that entropy specifically is a weak one. It does not establish anything about *unsupervised anomaly* admission, where the error mode is not a wrong pseudo-label but a true fault being written into normality.

### F10 — Replay-based and replay-free continual anomaly detection

Replay work is almost entirely task-incremental with clean per-task normal data: [ReplayCAD (IJCAI 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06603) synthesises old-task samples with a diffusion model guided by compressed semantic embeddings; [ONER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03907) replays image- and pixel-level prototypes; [CADIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08634) maintains a single shared incremental coreset; [DeCoFlow (ECCV 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-37092-1_5) decomposes normalizing flows with prototype-based routing and no replay; [ReCATS](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817985) is the replay-free MTSAD instance. These establish forgetting-mitigation under known task boundaries. They do not address admission at all — the data entering each task's training set is defined by the benchmark, not decided by the method.

### F11 — Normality-preserving / anti-forgetting continual anomaly detection

The most directly named instance is [normality-preserving continual industrial AD via orthogonal LoRA banks (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02042), which projects layer activations onto the orthogonal complement of previously used subspaces so new-task updates do not overwrite old normality. [UCAD (AAAI 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01010) does unsupervised continual AD with a contrastively learned key-prompt-knowledge memory; [neural-collapse-guided task-free continual AD (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03406) uses fixed ETF prototypes to avoid task boundaries; [a systematic continual-AD framework and benchmark (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18289) formalises inter-task change as a shift in the *normal* distribution. Note the assumption these share and the project does not: per-task normal data is given as clean, taken directly from ground-truth labels. That is precisely the assumption the admission question is about.

### F12 — Novelty detection and open-set / open-world anomaly detection

The conceptual framework is settled by the [generalized OOD survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11334) (covariate versus semantic shift; clean-ID assumption). [Open-Set MTSAD (ECAI 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12294) assumes labelled disjoint normal and anomaly sets, which is a different supervision regime from the project's. [Anomaly detection under distribution shift (ICCV 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13845) adapts a detector to shifted test distributions using teacher–student feature agreement. [AnoShift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.15476) provides the shift benchmark. The family establishes the taxonomy the project needs to use when it says "legitimate new normal" (a covariate shift within the normal class) but supplies no timestamp-level drift truth for time series.

### F13 — Continual-learning memory mechanisms

Dual-memory designs are well established outside AD: [DualNet (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00175) pairs a slow representation learner with a fast learner, and [CLS-ER (ICLR 2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12604) maintains plastic and stable exponential-moving-average copies of the working model. Inside AD, memory takes the form of prototypes, coresets and codebooks ([UCAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01010), [CADIC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08634), [COMET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01635)). The family establishes stable/plastic separation as an architecture; it does not establish a *gating policy* for what enters the plastic store under unsupervised, possibly-contaminated streaming input, which is the version the project would need.

## Internal state as evidence, and how the field measures success

### F6 — Recurrent hidden / gating / memory state as confidence, uncertainty or shift evidence

This is the thinnest family in the map and, for the project's current probe, the most important. Targeted searches for recurrent gate or cell-state dynamics used as drift or shift evidence in TSAD returned essentially nothing: `abs:"LSTM" AND abs:"internal state" AND abs:"drift detection"` and `abs:"hidden state" AND abs:"anomaly detection" AND abs:"time series" AND abs:"latent"` both returned zero arXiv records. What exists is adjacent rather than on-target: gate activation signals correlate with phoneme boundaries in speech ([2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07588)); a modified forget gate / self-contained cell state is used for video anomaly estimation ([2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01478)); LoRA gate activations are read out as a diagnostic of when adaptation engages ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19028)); a state-space detector's learned latent dynamics drive incremental adaptation ([iADCPS 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04374)); a Kalman-style filter's standardised innovation is used as a nonconformity score ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30502)); and MemStream's memory contents serve as the scoring and update-gating signal ([2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03837)). Two caveats on this apparent gap: the two closest items were read at abstract level only, and "internal state" is used very broadly in the wider TTA literature — entropy, logits, Fisher information, BN statistics, prototypes and latent distances are all internal quantities, and *those* are thoroughly mined (F7, F9, F15). The gap is specific: recurrent gate/memory dynamics, in TSAD, evaluated for incremental information about drift versus anomaly.

### F16 — Industrial streaming deployment and evaluation-methodology critiques

The evaluation critique is unusually well developed and constrains how any result in this project may be reported. Point-adjustment inflates scores to the point where random scores can beat trained detectors ([Kim et al., AAAI 2022](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v36i7.20680)); the broader methodology critique covers dataset triviality and protocol leakage ([Wagner et al., TPCTC 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.13068)); the metric space itself has been taxonomised ([Sørbø & Ruocco, DMKD 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10618-023-00988-8)); range-based precision/recall ([NeurIPS 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03639)) and VUS-family measures ([2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13318)) are the recommended replacements; and a 2026 adversarial stress-test asks whether the post-point-adjustment fixes actually fixed it ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11969)). Benchmarks named by later work include TSB-AD ([10.52202/079017-3437](https://doi.org/10.52202/079017-3437), retrieved as metadata only) and TAB ([PVLDB 2025](https://doi.org/10.14778/3746405.3746407), abstract withheld), with MSAD ([VLDB J 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00778-025-00949-1)) on model selection and [StreamAD](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tbench.2023.100121) on online cloud-metric benchmarking. Entry points: Kim et al. 2022, Wagner et al. 2023, Sørbø & Ruocco 2023.

## Closest prior art

Ordered by how much of the project's intended contribution each one already occupies. Confidence refers to my overlap assessment, not to the paper's own quality.

**1. SCALE — Style-Causal Disentanglement with Adaptive Lifelong Expert ([10.1145/3770855.3817912](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817912), 2026, abstract only).** *Already does:* poses online single-pass anomaly detection with unobserved domain labels and shift times and a growing number of domains; separates domain drift from true anomalies using two decoupled model-derived criteria (Sparse Likelihood Regret for drift and expert routing, Counterfactual Prediction Residual for anomaly scoring), with claimed theoretical support; adapts continually via an expandable expert pool. *Does not do (as far as the abstract shows):* no deferral or quarantine of a candidate normal, no reversal of an adaptation, no measurement of contamination harm, and no treatment of recurrent internal dynamics as the evidence source. **Threat: high. Confidence: medium** — abstract-level reading only; the full text could either strengthen the overlap (if it evaluates separation against timestamp truth) or weaken it (if the separation is asserted by construction).

**2. CANDI ([10.1609/aaai.v40i17.38524](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i17.38524), AAAI 2026, full text read).** *Already does:* model-internal admission control for a TSAD adaptation buffer — latent Mahalanobis distance to high-scoring normal validation windows plus a score threshold decides which windows are used for adaptation; preserves pre-trained knowledge by updating only a residual module. *Does not do:* no drift-versus-anomaly decision, no deferral, no rollback (explicitly future work), and contamination is acknowledged and tolerated rather than prevented or costed. **Threat: high for the admission component, low for the rest. Confidence: high.**

**3. HTM + SPRT hybrid framework for real-time drift and anomaly identification ([10.33889/IJMEMS.2025.10.3.039](https://doi.org/10.33889/IJMEMS.2025.10.3.039), 2025, full text read).** *Already does:* binarises a detector's own internal anomaly/likelihood output and runs a sequential probability ratio test over a lagged cumulative window to declare drift onset, restarting the test after each decision — i.e. a delayed, statistically-controlled drift declaration built from internal evidence, explicitly separating drift from transient anomaly. *Does not do:* does not adapt a deep detector's parameters as a consequence, does not handle multivariate learned recurrent models, does not measure contamination harm, and is not evaluated against the TSAD evaluation critiques. **Threat: high for the deferred-test component. Confidence: high.**

**4. When the Martingale Never Stops Firing — anytime-valid gating ([arXiv:2608.30502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30502), 2026, full text read).** *Already does:* uses a filter's own standardised innovation as the nonconformity score of a conformal test martingale; maps the test state onto a four-way action table (normal / huberize / skip / sustained drift); resets the martingale on fire and applies a covariance response to drift; includes a delayed-feedback ablation revealing labels 96 steps late. This is drift-versus-anomaly triage from internal state, with anytime validity and an explicit skip (non-commit) action. *Does not do:* four-page preprint on forecast streams with a linear filter; no learned recurrent detector, no anomaly-detection quality evaluation, and it states the gate does not prevent contamination from affecting adaptation. **Threat: high as a construct precedent, low as a competing result. Confidence: high.**

**5. COMET ([arXiv:2602.01635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01635), 2026, full text read).** *Already does:* threshold-free identification of reliable normal samples for online adaptation from *which codebook entries were activated during normal-only training*, with the codebook updated only on those samples explicitly "to prevent codebook contamination"; positions itself against M2N2's threshold-based filtering. *Does not do:* no drift-versus-anomaly test, no deferral, no rollback, no causal contamination accounting, and the internal evidence is a quantised-embedding activation pattern, not recurrent gate/memory dynamics. **Threat: high for "internal state as admission evidence is new". Confidence: high.**

**6. METER ([10.14778/3636218.3636233](https://doi.org/10.14778/3636218.3636233), PVLDB 2023, full text read).** *Already does:* evidential (Dirichlet) concept uncertainty per sample decides routing between a static detector and a dynamically generated one, and an offline updating strategy accumulates that uncertainty over a sliding window before committing an update — uncertainty-gated adaptation with accumulate-then-commit. *Does not do:* the uncertainty is over the detector's output, not over drift-versus-anomaly as distinct hypotheses; no reversal; no contamination accounting; initial training assumes anomaly-free history. **Threat: medium-high. Confidence: high.**

**7. RDumb++ ([arXiv:2601.15544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15544), 2026, full text read) and adaptive selective reset ([arXiv:2603.03796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03796), ICLR 2026, full text read).** *Already do:* detect drift from the model's own signals (entropy, KL divergence, prediction concentration and its EMA) and trigger full or partial parameter resets exactly when drift is detected, with explicit analysis of premature versus delayed resets. *Do not do:* classification CTTA with a fixed source model to revert to; no notion of a legitimate new normal that must survive the reset, no anomaly task, no contamination accounting. **Threat: high for "reset-on-drift-evidence". Confidence: high.**

**8. MemStream ([arXiv:2106.03837](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03837), WWW 2022, full text read).** *Already does:* gates memory updates by a discounted KNN-based anomaly score threshold so that high-scoring records do not enter memory; replaces memory contents under drift; proves a relation between memory size and tolerable drift. *Does not do:* no drift-versus-anomaly decision as such, no deferral, no learned recurrent internals, univariate/streaming-record framing. **Threat: medium. Confidence: high.**

**9. Safety-Gated Continual Learning for Drift-Aware Anomaly Detection in Real-Time Cyber-Physical Systems ([10.1109/icaiset66439.2026.11541767](https://doi.org/10.1109/icaiset66439.2026.11541767), 2026, metadata only).** *Already does — possibly:* the title names a safety-gated continual-learning update for drift-aware anomaly detection, which is the project's mechanism family in one phrase; it cites M2N2. *Unknown:* everything else. No abstract or full text was retrievable this session. **Threat: unresolved, potentially high. Confidence: very low** — this is the single largest unquantified novelty risk in the map and should be read before any novelty claim is written.

**10. STAD ([10.1016/j.datak.2024.102365](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.datak.2024.102365), DKE 2024, abstract only).** *Already does:* statistical drift tests (with sensitivity/power analysis) drive transitions between discrete per-state normality models, making adaptation under drift interpretable and separating drift decisions from anomaly scoring. *Does not do:* no deferral of a candidate normal pending evidence, no reversal, no internal recurrent dynamics, no contamination accounting. **Threat: medium. Confidence: medium** — abstract only.

## What would remain novel

Stated as four separable claims, each with the specific prior art it has to clear. None of these presumes the project's current probe returns a positive result; each is phrased so it remains meaningful if the probe's outcome is null, because a well-powered null on the information question is itself a contribution against the assumption that internal dynamics obviously help.

**1. Internal-state evidence for drift versus anomaly, as a measurement.** Prior art uses internal quantities as *gates* (entropy, Fisher, BN statistics, latent distance, codebook activation, evidential uncertainty, filter innovation) and asserts their adequacy by downstream task performance. What is not in the retrieved literature is the prior question treated as a measurement: given frozen detectors and evaluator-side truth about which changes are drift and which are anomalies, how much incremental information do internal summaries carry *beyond score-and-history controls*? The novelty here is the controlled comparison — the control set, the frozen-detector design that removes adaptation as a confound, and the willingness to report a null. It has to clear F7/F9/F15 on "internal signals are useful" (already established) by not claiming that, and it has to clear the anytime-valid gating preprint on "internal state can feed a drift-versus-anomaly decision" (already established as a construct) by measuring rather than constructing.

**2. Model-specific (xLSTM) internal-state evidence.** Nothing retrieved connects xLSTM's exponential gating or matrix memory to drift, anomaly, or adaptation. A capacity-matched comparison against an LSTM on the same streams, with the same probe and the same controls, would be the first published evidence either way about whether the architecture's gate/memory dynamics carry information a plain recurrent memory does not. This is the narrowest claim in the set and the one least threatened by prior art — but it is also the one whose value depends most on the controls, because a difference between two backbones on a synthetic stream is not yet a statement about deployed detectors. The relevant risk is not prior art but external validity.

**3. Delayed / quarantined commitment with causal contamination accounting.** Deferral mechanisms exist (METER's accumulate-then-commit, SPRT-over-internal-output, Quilt's post-drift wait, pending-transition buffers, verification-latency formalisms) and admission guards exist (M2N2's masking, COMET's codebook evidence, MemStream's threshold, CANDI's latent curation). What is absent is the accounting: no retrieved paper measures the *causal* cost of admitting a true anomaly into a normality buffer under a prespecified harm margin, and the closest anchor, CANDI, explicitly reports contamination as present and argues only for robustness to it. A contribution here is an evaluation contract — controlled contamination interventions, a prespecified margin, and a reported effect with its uncertainty — rather than a new gate. Note that this claim is about what is *measured*, so it survives a null on claims 1 and 2.

**4. The combination as a deployable mechanism.** Delayed, reversible normality adaptation in which admission is gated by measured internal evidence, commitment is deferred until a sequential test resolves, and a commitment can be reversed without discarding a legitimately learned new normal. Each component is occupied separately; the combination is not, and specifically the reset semantics are not — every reset mechanism retrieved here reverts toward a *fixed source model* rather than reversing one normality commitment while preserving others. The honest caveat is that "not yet combined" is the weakest form of novelty, and the combination only becomes a contribution if the components it combines are shown to be load-bearing. On current evidence the load-bearing question (claim 1) is open, and the design-space survey should be written on the assumption that it may resolve negatively.
