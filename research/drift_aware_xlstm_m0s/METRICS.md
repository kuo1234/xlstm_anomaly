# Quantities, units and gate interpretation

Loss per suffix offset k and paired realization is channel-mean squared one-step residual in **training-scaled coordinates**. Within a checkpoint every arm uses the same scaler, exact targets and timestamps. Model seeds have their own training scaler; normalized effects are computed per seed before group aggregation. Native output at input t forecasts target t+1; loss is never same-input reconstruction.

Primary H(k)=loss(incompatible KEEP)−loss(compatible B KEEP). R(k)=loss(incompatible KEEP)−loss(oracle RESET). A seed's primary H/R are means over 8 paired suffix realizations and k32..63, divided by compatible KEEP mean MSE in that interval. Recovery fraction=R/H when H>0; otherwise undefined (JSON null). Normalized long-context benefit over k32..255 = (compatible FIXED-L−compatible KEEP)/compatible KEEP. Replay-vs-reset primary difference is normalized by primary compatible MSE. Stationary deterioration uses physically continuous A and B trajectories; positive KEEP−RESET in both cannot be dismissed as drift-specific harm.

Offset curves preserve each run's replicate-mean values; bins0..7,8..15,16..31,32..63,64..127,128..255 are predeclared. Descriptive effect onset is first unsmoothed mean offset H≥.05; peak is max nonnegative H, half-life is first later offset at/below half peak; sustained recovery is first post-peak start of16 consecutive |H|≤.01. None exists → null; sustained recovery beyond256 is censored. No smoothing/peak choice controls a gate. A noisy isolated crossing can affect descriptive half-life, so it is not called a reliable decay constant.

Scientific unit is **five physical groups per mechanism**. Seeds11/22/33 and eight process realizations are repeated paired measurements. Report every group mean/seed range, group median/range and sign pattern; no iid window p-values, no model-seed bootstrap and no confidence interval presented from the plotted group range. Shared group transforms across mechanisms mean mechanisms cannot simply be pooled as15 independent sources.

B-support is held-out compatible B forecasting adequacy. Both simple baselines consume observations strictly before the target. Linear AR is trained on independent stationary B training data only, with no test fitting. Native regime truth is not supplied to a recurrent model. B-support thresholds and seed/group witnesses are in PROTOCOL.md.

Point AUPRC is specifically **average precision**, grouped by equal score thresholds (ties preserved), `sum(delta_recall * precision)`. It is not trapezoidal PR-AUC or point-adjusted AP. Event recall is fraction of injected events with at least one alarm within their true interval; no future extension. Normal false alarm rate counts normal suffix timestamps exceeding the fixed calibration threshold; burden per1000=1000×FPR. Post-reset missed anomaly-point rate is fraction of anomalous suffix points below/equal threshold. Mean anomaly-score attenuation reports RESET/KEEP on identical anomalous points; it is descriptive and does not replace detection metrics. AP with no positives would be null, though every gated event condition includes positives.

Boundary oracle reset is not a deployed decision. Its first score at offset0 equals KEEP's because the forecast predates the boundary. Collective anomalies are not excluded from input/state: their observed values can be absorbed on following steps, which is the explicit diagnostic. Labels/events are consumed only by the evaluator. No anomaly labels tune architecture/checkpoint/threshold/onsets/amplitude or task difficulty. Every predeclared type/onset occurs for both target regimes independently of A/B identities.

Verdicts are operational falsification decisions at frozen practical margins, not significance or equivalence proofs. Inconclusive context control explicitly withholds selective-memory interpretation. Gates require joint seed witnesses and physical-group reproducibility. No metric or hard group may be dropped to rescue a negative verdict.
