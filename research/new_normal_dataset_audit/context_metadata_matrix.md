## Interpretation

The clearest context metadata are in E-Energy (schedule/setpoint semantics), PreDist (service events and configuration), TEP (controlled modes and fault injections), NoBOOM (cause observability and post-removal effect), Batch Distillation (recipe/run journal), C-MAPSS (operating settings), and DAYPSCI (external system state). Rich context alone does not establish the specific ground truth M6 needs. In particular, PreDist's safe intervals and E-Energy's row classes do not by themselves provide old-regime recurrence and event-level persistence labels.

For any follow-up, retain raw timestamps, entity/run IDs, context channels, source split, label provenance, and event censoring. Do not collapse “no recorded fault” into “known benign.”
