# Next decision

The strong observable-control result is exploratory and positive for both
backbones under a linear probe.  The next most informative experiment should
be a separately frozen **nonlinear observable-only probe stress test** (with
the same rows, folds, and residual basis) before any move toward online safe
adaptation.  This is the remaining direct threat to the linear-accessibility
interpretation.

The post-G1 mLSTM dense mechanism line may then receive its planned multi-seed
replication, but it must remain separate from this common-recurrent result.
Neither outcome authorizes W128/W256, persistent-state testing, H3b, or online
adaptation.  Any nonlinear probe must be bounded, predeclared, and clearly
reported as a new exploratory study rather than appended to the G1 family.
