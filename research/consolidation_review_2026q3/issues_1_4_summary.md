## Issue #3 — `IDENTIFIABILITY_BOUNDARY_ESTABLISHED`

Time series alone cannot in general distinguish benign new normal from persistent
fault under observational equivalence. Quarantine and promotion are risk management,
not semantic identification. This is the canonical theoretical boundary for future
M6 work.

## Issue #4 — `M1_REPRODUCIBILITY_AUDIT_BLOCKED`

No direct source-level label leakage into training, forecasting, scoring, calibration,
or fusion was found. All 28 SMD labels had previously been parsed for descriptive
manifest statistics, so the “other 19 globally untouched” confirmatory claim is
invalid. The blocker concerns label-exposure provenance and strict confirmatory
status; it is not evidence that labels entered training.
