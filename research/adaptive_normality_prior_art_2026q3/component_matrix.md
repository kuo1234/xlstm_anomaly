## Matrix interpretation

No single column is a novelty claim. “Protected state” spans frozen weights, replay, immutable banks and architecture boundaries, which are not interchangeable. “Promotion” must name a transition from tentative evidence to a confirmed normal regime, not merely an online optimizer step or insertion below a threshold. The matrix suggests that the plausible distinction is the combination of state-level isolation and a lifecycle with explicit false-promotion/recovery measures, under a narrower recurrent TSAD scope.
