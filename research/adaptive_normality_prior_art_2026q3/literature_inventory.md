## Coverage gaps and confidence

The strongest direct overlap is across several papers rather than a single complete duplicate: M2N2 and CANDI cover normality-gated test-time adaptation; MemStream covers score-gated normal-memory admission; METER accumulates unknown/uncertain cases before updating; AnDri covers candidate normal patterns and recurring-pattern reactivation; ARCUS and O-LoRA cover old-model or old-bank retention; the smart-building method covers a persistent drift buffer with selective updates. That makes a claim based on mere combination weak.

The review is not a systematic review or exhaustive search. It does not establish exhaustive database coverage, publication-priority, or exact nonexistence of a method. The largest unresolved near-cutoff lead is SCALE. Safety-Gated CL and MemTTA have incomplete detail. Recheck these and search proceedings/citation chains before making a priority claim.

## Inference boundaries

- “A complete temporal quarantine-to-promotion lifecycle was not located in the audited recurrent TSAD papers” is a bounded observation about this inventory.
- “No one has done this” is not supported.
- The utility of a recurrent state representation is an empirical question against matched history encoders, finite windows, and residual baselines.
- Drift and anomaly semantics cannot generally be separated from a time series alone when their observable distributions are identical.
