# Six-claim red-team audit

Exact allowed status labels are used below.

| # | Claim | Status | Evidence and reasoning | Defensible replacement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Existing test-time adaptation mostly changes parameters rather than protected recurrent state. | **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** | M2N2 adapts model parameters using predicted-normal test points. CANDI adapts residual parameters while freezing the backbone and latent space. CoTTA restores source weights during parameter adaptation. METER updates model parameters after uncertainty accumulates. These are strong representative examples, but “mostly” requires a scoped quantitative survey. MemTTA adds a neural normal-pattern memory, and MD-RS/AnDri/ARCUS address recurrent states, patterns, or model pools in adjacent settings. | “In the inspected TSAD TTA methods M2N2 and CANDI, adaptation is parameter-based rather than promotion of persistent recurrent normal-state regimes.” |
| 2 | Existing memory methods do not quarantine uncertain observations before regime promotion. | **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** | MemStream admits an embedding only after a score threshold. CANDI filters probable false positives against a normal reference bank. METER outputs unknown and accumulates uncertainty in a sliding window before a window-based update. Smart-building AD buffers repeated predicted drift. AnDri constructs candidate patterns and admits coherent patterns as normal. Thus filtering, deferral and candidate admission are individually known. In the inspected sources no exact recurrent TSAD system documents the entire temporal quarantine → candidate regime → confirmed promotion → rollback lifecycle. Absence from this finite audit is not evidence of global novelty. | Claim only the specific stateful lifecycle, if implemented and compared: “We evaluate explicit time-based quarantine and promotion criteria for a recurrent normal-regime bank, including contamination and recovery tradeoffs.” |
| 3 | Existing TSAD does not maintain multiple protected/recoverable normal regimes. | **NOT_SUPPORTED** | AnDri explicitly supports active/inactive temporal patterns and reactivation. ARCUS maintains and reuses a pool of concept-specific models. O-LoRA banks retain old normality by category (cross-domain). These defeat the broad claim, even though they are not exact recurrent TSAD duplicates. | Restrict to the representation and transition rule, and acknowledge pattern/model-pool precedents: persistent recurrent state, with specified isolation and recurrence matching, in a TSAD stream. |
| 4 | xLSTM is uniquely suitable for the proposed mechanism. | **NOT_SUPPORTED** | xLSTM is a recurrence architecture. It does not itself supply safe adaptation, anomaly/drift semantics, quarantine, or old-regime protection. LSTM/GRU, switching RNNs, state-space models, reservoirs and finite-history encoders are plausible alternatives. No inspected comparison establishes exclusive suitability. | Treat xLSTM as one implementation substrate and compare against at least one simpler recurrent and one non-recurrent history representation under matched capacity and update rules. |
| 5 | Persistent hidden state adds information beyond observable history. | **NOT_SUPPORTED** | For a deterministic recurrent update, hidden state at time t is a function of the input history through t (and initialization/parameters). It cannot carry information beyond the full observed history. MD-RS supports state as a potentially useful statistic compared with selected scoring baselines, not information-theoretic superiority over complete history. | “A recurrent state may be a computationally useful compressed summary of history compared with a fixed-window or residual baseline.” Test with matched history access, reset conditions, and capacity. |
| 6 | Benign new-normal behavior and persistent anomaly can be identified from time series alone. | **NON_IDENTIFIABLE** | If a benign process shift and a fault produce the same observed sequence distribution, no time-series-only detector can assign their semantic labels reliably in general. Temporal persistence, recurrence, coherence and reconstruction fit are assumptions/heuristics, not semantic identification. AnDri also discusses the intrinsic ambiguity and contamination risk. | State assumptions and use external context where available: operator confirmation, process constraints, exogenous variables, maintenance events, or a declared risk policy. Without these, report uncertainty and defer irreversible promotion. |

## Implication

The strongest defensible gap is a testable systems question about whether explicit quarantine, candidate evidence accumulation, protected recurrent regimes, promotion, and recurrence-based reuse improve the contamination-versus-recovery frontier under stated assumptions. This is a mechanism and evaluation claim, not a universal guarantee that the system can know which persistent change is benign.
