# Prior-art overlap — bounded method/code audit

Broad claims that operating context, normal-condition transfer, selective updates, knowledge preservation or validation before deployment are new are untenable. The narrower candidate is causal, non-oracle authorization evidence for unknown-regime reference admission, with unresolved outcomes and reversible complete state. Novelty of that intersection is **UNRESOLVED**, not established.

## Novelty decision and falsification

Do not claim that context universally solves fault-vs-benign separation. If context is f(X), it carries no incremental information conditional on X. If identical X and available C remain possible under both truths, a universal separator remains impossible. Authorization can constrain permitted intent while a fault coexists.

The potential contribution must be evaluated against (a) ordinary conditional monitoring, (b) selective TTA and (c) validation-gated continual deployment with the SAME information/arrival budget. Stronger comparison may be an auditable causal benchmark/error contract rather than a new learner. Full ConCord and validation-gated method details remain review blockers for method novelty. PRIOR_ART_SATURATED is not established, but its risk is substantial; absence of accessible full text cannot count as positive novelty evidence.
