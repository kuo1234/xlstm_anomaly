# Validate evidence: research decision

Authorized [review5978818835](https://github.com/kuo1234/xlstm_anomaly/issues/15#issuecomment-5978818835). Step2f protocol PASS / limited diagnostic PASS; historical artifacts unchanged. This decision precedes new pilot outcomes. No predetermined Step2g requirement. Work/bytes remain ssh kuo.

The failure mechanism is **legitimacy aliasing under passive observation**, with scalar compression as a demonstrated contributor. Closed-loop control can settle after either a command change or a persistent fault. A stable forecastable regime need not be healthy. Step2e absorbed both long faults; Step2f clipping did not explain the alias. Residual concentration has signal in five cases, but the reverse diagnostic probe fails across every seed/operator. More capacity cannot by itself supply missing semantics.

Information lost by norm/CV includes which channels carry energy, its sign, coordination/lag and controller-response context. Preserving these is promising, but source evidence of a legal operating change is distinct from measuring statistical consistency. If healthy and fault trajectories have the same observation distribution, no observation-only representation can identify their legitimacy without assumptions. This is a mathematical limitation, not a claim that these particular TEP trajectories are identical.

## Choice

**First: threshold-free structural transfer stress test (Pilot P1).** This choice is conditional on current evidence, not reviewer naming Step2g. It provides the highest immediate information per intervention: determine whether the only stable finding survives new native seeds/fault mechanisms before spending normal-training effort or designing Verify. Six literal runs, no new features, no classifier, no admission. Reuse frozen Step2f R0 extraction. An independent fault family that does not concentrate energy can directly falsify the proposed ordering; a new benign seed can show that the original finding was shared-prefix artefact. Both outcomes are useful.

This alone cannot solve core aliasing; it tests one observation-based premise. A good outcome only motivates a prospective rule/context study, not safe PROMOTE. A bad outcome stops this route immediately; do not then select a different feature, tune a threshold, replace faults, or start another pilot in the same task. Write a decision update for review. Ramp and seed change together in published SP runs: this is a joint stress test, not a causal seed ablation.

Do not start learned models: Step2f has not shown that a learned history adds new information, and normal prefix319points is insufficient training/support. Do not start graph/coherence: healthy command excitation/system identification needs a distinct manifest and assumptions. Do not start LEFT fusion: frequency consistency is an untested prior, not demonstrated semantic separation. Do not start full Verify: shadow prediction may reward stable faults; Step1c is an oracle reversibility result. Context is ultimately relevant but cannot be substituted for source-state labels in this observation-only pilot.

## Literature: evidence vs inference

[Reinartz et al. official DTU publication](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/an-extended-tennessee-eastman-simulation-dataset-for-fault-detect/) establishes an extended simulator dataset for fault detection/decision support. Author thesis Appendix A.3.1/A.3.2 explicitly includes feasible operating variations and30-40-30 split, supporting source SP semantics; not evidence that a detector may safely admit them.

[Hinder et al., unsupervised drift survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15826) distinguishes detection/localization/explanation of distribution changes. Our inference: discovering a change cannot establish operational legitimacy.

[LEFT v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08638v2) proposes complementary time/frequency/multiscale representations and trained cycle consistency. This motivates a candidate mechanism; its TSAD abstract does not validate stable-benign admission. Fourier/inverse identity alone would be vacuous consistency evidence.

Repo evidence: Step2e TRANSFER_NOT_SUPPORTED, Step2f limited structural PASS/reverse-probe failure; Step1c oracle rollback requires fault-touched write provenance and W3 replay. None supplies automatic legitimacy supervision.
